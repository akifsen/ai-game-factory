## Change

The legacy GLB validator now builds one frozen decoded context and evaluates six
closed ordered groups: mesh structure, box collider, budgets, dimensions/origin/
snap, LOD bounds and orientation. The facade preserves parse/hash chronology and
the historical supported-exception boundary. Low-level GLB inspection and
preflight remain unchanged. Unsupported V0.7 specifications are rejected
explicitly; advanced geometry and production profiles remain pending.

Plans reject unknown groups/capabilities, empty plans, duplicates and noncanonical
order. This last guard resolves the independent review's only actionable finding.
The document wrapper is shallow read-only; existing pure rules do not mutate its
nested parser data. World matrices and decoded geometry use immutable tuples.

The merge at initial HEAD `68e32e9` had reintroduced four duplicate golden files.
Those are removed. Authoritative Phase A goldens remain byte unchanged, and the
unique forbidden-content coverage already exists in focused tests.
