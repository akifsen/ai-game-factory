an immutable evidence container with insert-only manifest/result/marker registrations
on the held SQLite writer connection. Publication rechecks the current TEST_ONLY
receipt, source bindings, execution identity and exact container bytes, then performs
a same-volume rename that refuses to overwrite an existing destination.

runs the fixed packaged read-only `python -I` cold verifier exactly once before the
writer lock, and rechecks authoritative upstream state and evidence bytes under the
lock. Stored and fresh cold results must match canonically. Production and promotion
eligibility remain false; public `rigged_character` remains unsupported.

## Installed-wheel CI preflight

The candidate CI builds one pure `gamefactory-0.8.0rc1` wheel and shares its exact
SHA256 and source HEAD with 16 unit consumers: four frozen shards on Ubuntu and
Windows with Python 3.11 and 3.12. Every consumer checks its collected test identities
against the checked-in 425-node inventory before execution, then reconciles actual
JUnit testcase identities and statuses with collection. Only three explicit Windows
junction probes may skip on Linux. Ordinary CI excludes `candidate_slow`; plain
local pytest retains those tests.

The separate six-test candidate Blender/Godot gate collects and executes under Xvfb.
It asserts the pinned Godot version, probes Blender's NumPy runtime, and archives
the four selected publication JSON files with their hashes. Each consumer archives
its own source provenance separately from the builder's. The historical six-test
rig/skin gate and its existing wheel flow remain unchanged.

Independent preflight verification passed 31 helper cases, including real isolated
venv import probes. The final shard CLI revision passed its three affected cases
(the existing roundtrip and two new exact-set cases). Ruff check/format passed on
252 Python files; mypy passed on 127 production files and the two CI helper files.
YAML, the 16-job matrix, the canonical 425 identities, and unchanged historical jobs
were checked independently. The real workflow evidence-copy hook passed with
Blender and Godot: one test, zero skips, 67.45 seconds; all four JSON files were
present and the copied trusted cold outcome matched the publication result.

These local results are preflight evidence, not a claim that remote installed-wheel
CI or the final release gate has passed.
