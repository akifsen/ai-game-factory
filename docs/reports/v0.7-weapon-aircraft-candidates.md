# V0.7 weapon and aircraft profile candidates

These are closed, test-local V0.7 assembly candidates. They are not added to
the builtin registry and do not advertise product availability.

`weapon@1` binds the `local_operator_assembly` source kind to a receiver,
barrel, grip, and optional bolt. The receiver, barrel, and grip are fixed; the
bolt may translate on local +Z. The required muzzle frame sits at the barrel's
forward end and faces root −Z. The grip-contact frame is declared at the grip's
forward end and faces root −Y, which is its intended downward hand-contact
direction. This separate socket orientation is checked against both GLB and
Godot data; it does not change the weapon's root forward direction.

`aircraft@1` describes a fixed fuselage, full-span wing, and tailplane plus a
rotating two-blade propeller. The propeller rotates around local +Z, parallel
to the aircraft's longitudinal axis (the sign of a rotation axis does not
change the axis line). The fixture uses an 11 m span, a 7.84 m depth,
and a 2.4 m height; it makes no flight or aerodynamic claim. Both candidates
require a static body, root box collider/ray hit, LOD0 and generated LOD1,
unrigged/unanimated geometry, and the exact nine review views.

The aircraft framing target is `.32` while the other profile defaults remain
`.20` minimum, `.75` maximum, and `.04` viewport margin. Real Godot output at
the inherited `.48` target framed the nose/rear views at 48% of viewport height
but placed the wide wing bounds outside the viewport margin. Reducing only the
profile's target to `.32` zooms out those end-on views without relaxing the
runtime margin, allowed range, or geometry checks. At that target the real
Godot run passed all nine captures, collider ray test, propeller-axis motion,
rigid descendant movement, and rest-pose restoration.

The isolated real workflow fixture supplies explicit test-only concept, source,
and final review approvals. It uses no live provider, production approval, or
paid-generation request. Blender generated LOD1 and the root box without
changing source dimensions or part pivots. The geometry validator's negative
fixtures independently require severity `FAIL` for wrong part parent, moved
pivot, motion axis, socket orientation, and socket placement. The two fixture
databases are also checked for zero rows in the five paid/accounting tables.

On 2026-09-30 both fresh local workflows completed through evidence export.
Each run used Blender and the paired Godot 4.7.2 executable, then passed the
source and packaged isolated cold-evidence checks with `PASS` and
`product_ready=true`. The focused real-runtime integration test also confirmed
the five paid/accounting tables remain empty. These results validate only the
test-local workflows and exported bundles; neither profile is builtin,
activated, or advertised as generally available.
